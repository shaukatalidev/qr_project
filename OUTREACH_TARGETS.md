## ⚠️ Read this before you start: the ceiling is lower than §5 assumed

§5 says "identify the top 15–20 'best QR code generator' articles." I ran the SERP. **That
many independent articles do not exist in this niche.**

Roughly 80% of page-one real estate for `best QR code generator`, `best free QR code
generator`, and `best QR code menu generator` is **owned by QR vendors themselves** — content
marketing on their own domains. They will never add a rival. Confirmed vendor-owned and
therefore dead on arrival:

`qrcodekit.com` · `pageloot.com` · `the-qrcode-generator.com` (TQRCG) · `qr-code-generator.com`
(Bitly) · `hovercode.com` · `qrforever.com` · `getdynamicqr.com` · `qrwink.com` · `qrstuff.com`
· `linkscan.org` · `qrmenugenerator.io` · `qruisine.com` · `shortpen.com` · `lunyb.com` ·
`u2l.ai` · `allinoneqr.in` · `blogx.thinkific.com` · `diljitpr.net` (PR distribution, not
editorial) · `streetinsider.com` (press-release syndication)

**Do not email any of these.** Recording them here so the next person doesn't re-find them.

What's left is **6 genuine outreach targets and 3 self-serve listings**. That is still worth
doing — going from 1 → 7 referring domains is the single biggest proportional jump available
to you — but calibrate: expect **2–4 placements from 6 emails**, not 15.

## Tier C — the India gap (unserved, and yours to take)

No independent Indian publication currently covers **QR code management SaaS**. Searches
surface only payment QR (BharatPe, Paytm) or global vendor blogs. Given India is **50 of your
62 clicks**, this is a real opening — but it is *digital PR*, not listicle outreach, so it
needs an angle rather than a "please add us" email:

- **YourStory**, **Inc42**, **StartupTalky** — product/startup features. They need a story: an
  Indian-built SaaS competing with US incumbents on a free tier, or original scan data.
- **Indian restaurant / retail trade press** — you shipped a `menu` QR type and QR menus are
  everywhere in India.

⚠️ These are **blocked on the stats hub** (checklist §6). Pitching them without original data
is a cold pitch with no hook. Build the data page first, then pitch these with it.
